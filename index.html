Form Library

Form 1040

Purpose: Main tax return form

When needed: Always required

Schedule A (Itemized Deductions)

Purpose: Claim itemized deductions instead of standard

When needed: If mortgage interest, property tax, charitable donations, or medical expenses exceed standard deduction

Schedule B (Interest and Dividends)

Purpose: Report interest and dividend income

When needed: If interest or dividend income over $1,500

Schedule C (Business Income)

Purpose: Report self-employment business income/expenses

When needed: If you have self-employment or freelance income

Schedule D (Capital Gains)

Purpose: Report capital gains and losses

When needed: If you sold stocks, crypto, real estate, or other investments

Schedule SE (Self-Employment Tax)

Purpose: Calculate Social Security and Medicare taxes on self-employment income

When needed: If net self-employment income over $400

Form 8962 (Premium Tax Credit)

Purpose: Calculate health insurance premium tax credit

When needed: If you received advance premium tax credits for marketplace health insurance
